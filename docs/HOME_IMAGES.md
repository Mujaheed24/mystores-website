# Home page images: what to find or generate

Put the files in `public/images/home/` with the exact names below. Missing files are skipped, so add them one at a time. Compress every photo to under 250 KB first (squoosh.app is free). Use JPG.

**Style to keep everything consistent:** warm natural light, real Nigerian everyday settings, bright colours, the subject in the middle (corners get rounded and cropped), no text, no logos, no brand names on products.

## Ideas, search words and AI prompts
**hero-1: the seller with her phone.** Search: "African woman small business smartphone", "Nigerian entrepreneur phone shop".
Prompt: *Photo of a smiling Nigerian woman in her 30s holding a smartphone in her small fabric shop, colourful Ankara rolls behind her, warm natural light, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos.*

**hero-2: hair and beauty.** Search: "African hair salon braids", "hairstylist Nigeria".
Prompt: *Photo of a Nigerian hairstylist braiding a client's hair in a bright small salon, candid, warm light, no text.*

**hero-3: fashion and tailoring.** Search: "African tailor Ankara fabric", "African fashion designer sewing".
Prompt: *Photo of a Nigerian tailor holding up a finished Ankara dress in a small workshop, soft window light, no text.*

**step-1: name your shop.** Search: "typing on smartphone", "small shop signboard".
Prompt: *Close-up photo of hands typing on a smartphone at a shop counter, warm light, shallow depth of field, no text on screen.*

**step-2: add your products.** Search: "photographing products with phone", "product photography smartphone table".
Prompt: *Photo of a seller taking a picture of perfume bottles on a table with her phone, bright natural light, no brand names.*

**step-3: share and sell on WhatsApp.** Search: "customer receiving package", "delivery rider package Africa", "chatting on phone smiling".
Prompt: *Photo of a happy customer in Nigeria receiving a parcel at her door from a delivery rider, daylight, no logos.*

**seller strip (6 photos, portrait):**
1. Fashion and fabrics: colourful fabric stall in a market
2. Hair and beauty: wigs, hair products or a makeup artist at work
3. Phones and gadgets: phone accessories counter, phone repair
4. Perfumes and oils: rows of perfume bottles, oud and incense (no brand names)
5. Shoes and bags: sandals and handbags on display
6. Food and snacks: puff-puff, bread or a small bakery counter

Search words: "Lagos market stall", "African market colourful", "perfume bottles shelf", "handbags display shop", "African bakery". Prompt pattern: *Photo of [item] displayed at a small shop in Nigeria, warm natural light, vibrant colours, vertical composition, no text, no logos.*

**cta-bg:** busy colourful market, blurred. Search: "African market crowd", "Lagos street market". It sits under a dark green overlay, so it can be rough or blurred.

**og (share picture):** make it yourself in Canva at 1200 x 630: green background, the words "Your shop. Your link. Your customers on WhatsApp." and a phone picture. Put the MyStores name large.

## Rules for using photos
- Free sites such as Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay allow commercial use, but **read the licence on each photo** and do not use photos showing recognisable brands or other people's logos.
- Photos of real, recognisable people need their permission. For people you photograph yourself, ask them to agree (a WhatsApp message is fine) and keep it.
- AI-generated photos are fine for decoration. Do not generate pictures of real or famous people.
- Do not use photos that suggest a seller or customer who does not exist as proof ("testimonial"). Use them as mood pictures only.
